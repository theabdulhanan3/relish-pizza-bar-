Meet The Owner

Waqas Bhatti

Founder & Owner — Relish Pizza Bar

Relish Pizza Bar ka safar aik hi soch se shuru hua: “har customer ko five star quality ka khana, local market ki qeemat par.” Waqas Bhatti ki nigrani mein hamari kitchen roz taza dough, imported cheese aur hand-picked ingredients istemaal karti hai.

Aaj Khanqah Sharif aur gird-o-nawah ke log hamare yahan family dinners, birthday parties aur late-night cravings ke liye aate hain — aur hum har order ko usi mohabbat se tayyar karte hain.
